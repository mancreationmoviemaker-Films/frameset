FILMMAKER TIPS

  • Camera FOV: Select any camera to adjust its focal length (18mm, 24mm, 35mm, 50mm, 85mm, 135mm) and observe the real optical field-of-view cone interactively.
  • Lighting Spread: Adjust beam spread angle, distance, and gel temperature (3200K, 5600K, etc.) to communicate your lighting setups to gaffers and DPs.
  • Actor & Camera Blocking: Use the Blocking tool to draw trajectory paths indicating movement from start to finish with arrows and directional cues.
  • Measurements: Use the Measure tool (M) to check physical distances between actors, cameras, and lights in meters or feet.
